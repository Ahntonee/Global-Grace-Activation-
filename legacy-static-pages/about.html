A decade of grace, carried across nations.

Global Grace Activation began as a single night of worship and has grown into a movement reaching over 25 nations — raising a generation that seeks God, walks in purpose, and influences the world.

Vision

A generation fully alive in God, activated for global impact.

We see a world where grace is understood not as a doctrine but as a daily reality — where believers everywhere are equipped to walk in their God-given destiny and carry transformation into their spheres of influence: family, business, government, media and the arts.

Mission

Ignite revival. Release destiny. Transform lives.

Every gathering, broadcast and outreach exists to bring people into a real encounter with God — through worship, the Word, prayer and community — and to send them out equipped to live out that encounter long after the lights go down.

  • ✓ Host an annual global gathering open to every nation
  • ✓ Stream every encounter free, to anywhere with a connection
  • ✓ Equip local churches with resources between gatherings
  • ✓ Fund missions & community projects through giving partners
History

How the move began

From one room of worshippers to a global broadcast reaching a thousand cities.

  • 2016
    The first gathering.

    A single night of worship in a rented hall in Lagos with fewer than 200 people in the room.

  • 2019
    Going national.

    GGA moved to Solution Arena and began partnering with churches across Nigeria.

  • 2021
    First global broadcast.

    The gathering streamed live for the first time, reaching viewers in 12 nations overnight.

  • 2024
    500,000 lives.

    Combined in-person and online attendance crossed half a million across a single weekend.

  • 2026
    25 nations and counting.

    GGA 2026 prepares to gather its largest crowd yet, with volunteer hubs in 25 nations.

Founder

Pastor Daniel Obi

Pastor Daniel Obi convened the first Global Grace Activation gathering in 2016 out of a conviction that a generation was waiting for a genuine encounter with God, not another program. He continues to lead the movement's vision and ministers at every annual gathering, alongside pastoring a local congregation in Lagos.

Statement of faith

What we believe

The Scriptures. We believe the Bible is the inspired, authoritative Word of God.

One God. We believe in one God, eternally existing as Father, Son and Holy Spirit.

Salvation. We believe salvation is a gift of grace received through faith in Jesus Christ.

The Holy Spirit. We believe in the present-day work and gifts of the Holy Spirit.

The Church. We believe the Church is God's family, called to worship, community and mission.

Eternity. We believe in the bodily resurrection and the eternal reign of Christ.
